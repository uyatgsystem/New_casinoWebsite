Refund&Cancellation Policy

At Spin Hub, we strive to provide a transparent and fair gaming experience. Please read our Refund & Cancellation Policy carefully before making any deposits or using our services.


No Refund on Gameplay Deposits

All deposits made to your account are final and non-refundable.
By depositing funds, you acknowledge that the funds will be used to participate in games of chance and skill, where outcomes are not guaranteed.


Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe an unauthorized transaction has occurred on your account, please contact us immediately at support@spinclub.me.
We will investigate and take appropriate action. Refunds for unauthorized transactions may be issued only after verification and approval.


Cancellation of Services

Since our service is digital and gameplay is initiated instantly, cancellations are not possible once a deposit is made.
You may choose not to participate in any games after depositing, but no refund will be issued.
